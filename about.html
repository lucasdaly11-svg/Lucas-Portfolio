A little more about me

Hi, I'm Lucas.

I'm an enthusiastic learner with a genuine interest in how technology works — from the physical components inside a PC to the software and AI tools changing how people work.

My approach

I like understanding how things work, not just getting them to work.

A lot of my interest in technology comes from curiosity. If something isn't working, I want to understand why. If I come across a tool I've never used, I enjoy researching it, experimenting and learning what it can do. I tend to learn most effectively through practical projects, testing ideas and solving real problems.

That approach has led me to build desktop computers, install operating systems, troubleshoot hardware and software issues for friends, and start developing a small web project of my own. More recently, I've been exploring how AI tools can support research, problem-solving and business processes.
